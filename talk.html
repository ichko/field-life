Field Life

Particle life as a grid of concentrations

tl;dr

  • A cellular automaton on a grid
  • Every cell holds a concentration of each colour
  • A matrix says which colours attract or repel each other
  • Mass only moves between neighbouring cells, so each colour keeps its total

Prior work

  • Particle Life Ventrella, J. Clusters. Mohr, T. Particle Life. ventrella.com/Clusters/intro.html
  • MaCE: General Mass Conserving Dynamics for Cellular Automata Papadopoulos, V. & Guichard, E. (2025). arXiv:2507.12306. arxiv.org/abs/2507.12306

MaCE mass-conserving cellular automata · Papadopoulos & Guichard (2025)

A grid of cells
each cell has some value
at each time step we compute a delta: how much we want to change that value, from local information
in the MaCE paper this delta is called the affinity, A
the affinities are normalised with a softmax over each cell's 3×3 neighbourhood
β sets how picky: low spreads the mass evenly, high sends it to the highest A
and each cell gets a contribution from the cells around it
every cell does this, all at once
mass only moves, so the total never changes
A can be anything: here, mass is drawn to nearby mass and to a drifting wave
\( \displaystyle \text{share}_{\,j} \;=\; \frac{e^{\beta A_j}}{\sum_{k \,\in\, 3\times3} e^{\beta A_k}} \)

Field Life

A grid
one channel per particle colour
each cell holds a value in every channel
blended additively
blended by dominance
each channel has a kernel: bells over distance, normalised to sum to 0
and there is an interaction matrix
convolving: how much of each colour is around, counted by its kernel
red's row of the matrix: pulled towards (+) or pushed from (−) each colour
summed: where red wants to go
force scales that; repel pushes mass out of crowded cells, whatever their colour
MaCE moves the mass along A; β sets how sharply
running, slowly
full speed, more mass
