Field Life

Particle life as a grid of concentrations

Abstract

Particle life is a swarm of coloured dots that attract or repel each other, depending on their colours. Field Life keeps the rules and drops the dots: each colour becomes an amount per cell on a grid. The amounts move between neighbouring cells without ever being created or destroyed, so every colour keeps its total.

The live field needs this folder served over http, e.g. python3 -m http.server
