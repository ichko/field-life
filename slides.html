Conserving mass

Giving, one cell at a time

A cell hands out everything it holds. Neighbours with a higher score A get more. This is MaCE.

share = eβA / Σneighbours eβA
β 1.00 · drag the A bars · in 2D, the same over 3×3

Particles as channels

Within reach

A dot adds up the dots within reach. A cell adds up the mass within reach, weighted by the same force curve.

dots → a push
cells → a score A, per channel
Ac = Σd M[c][d] · (K ∗ ρd)

Then MaCE moves each channel's mass toward its higher A.
