2 · Mass conservation

MaCE

Every cell splits its mass among its 3×3 neighbours, weighted by eβA at each.

sharej = eβAj / Σk∈3×3 eβAk

Shares sum to 1, so what leaves a cell arrives somewhere.

Papadopoulos & Guichard (2025), arXiv:2507.12306
