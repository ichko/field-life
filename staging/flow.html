What a cell is

Eighteen numbers. Two of them are a direction — one vector, the way the cell is facing. Eight are mass, eight kinds of stuff. Eight are genes, a tag riding on the mass. That is the whole state, against seventy-four on the many-headed page, and it is why this one runs where that one crawls.

One step

— Sense. A two-lobed kernel is turned to face the cell's own direction and read off the eight mass fields: three taps in each lobe, at angle either side of straight ahead, dist cells out, spread by width. The right lobe counts positive and the left negative, so what comes back is eight numbers saying this kind of stuff is more over there than over here. One more tap straight ahead gives eight more saying how much is in front. Because the eight fields are packed four to a texture, all of that is fourteen samples.

— Think. Those sixteen, plus the eight genes and the eight masses already in the cell, go into a small network. It answers with nine numbers: one turn, and eight preferences, one per neighbour.

— Steer. The direction rotates by the turn, capped at turn degrees.

— Move. The nine preferences — the eight the network gave plus a zero for staying put — are added to commit·cos(θ − φk), which leans them towards wherever the cell is now facing, and put through a softmax. That gives nine shares which sum to exactly one, and the cell hands its contents to its nine neighbours in those proportions. Every gram that leaves arrives. Mass and genes take the same shares, so a flow never loses its tag; the direction is carried too, averaged by how much mass each contributor brought.

What this costs you

A softmax spreads a cell's contents over up to nine neighbours every step, which is a diffusion. The many-headed page shifts by whole cells and has none. Turn commit up and the shares collapse onto one neighbour and the smearing mostly goes; turn it down and the field is a fog. That knob is the whole trade.

And one direction to a cell means two fronts meeting cancel. They cannot pass through each other the way they can when a cell keeps a heading per direction — there is nowhere to put the second answer. Watch two arms of the same structure collide and you will see it.

Handling it

Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, shift-drag to paint and alt-drag to erase. Space holds, R reseeds, N draws a new network, H hides the panel, F is fullscreen. The readout measures the mass on the GPU; it does not move off its opening value.
