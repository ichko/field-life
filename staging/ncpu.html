What you are looking at. Sixteen fields share the grid. Four hold the digit and never move — they are read, not pushed, which is the whole reason this works: a moving digit dissolves into a blur long before it could arrive anywhere. Eleven are chemicals doing the work. One is the pointer, the only channel anyone reads.

The answer is a vote, not a destination. The pointer does not travel to one region. It splits into lobes that light several at once, and the class is whichever lobe ends up heaviest — so a near-tie and a confident answer look very different in the bars on the right.

Nothing is created. Each channel hands out everything it holds to its own neighbourhood every tick, so the total in each never changes. Classifying is rearranging.
