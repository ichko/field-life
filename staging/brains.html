The state

Three things, and nothing else.

— Eight matter channels. Stuff lying on the ground. Deposited, spread and faded. Not conserved, and it must not be: fading is what makes a trail a trail.

— A direction. Two numbers, one vector, which way this cell is facing.

— Eight species channels. How much of each species is in this cell. Conserved, each one on its own.

The step

— Perceive. The kernel is turned to face the cell's own direction and convolved across all eight matter channels. Each of its two lobes gives a reading per channel, so what comes out is a perception vector of sixteen numbers. One kernel, one perception, shared by everybody in the cell.

— Think. Each species has a brain — a small network, sampled at random for now. Every brain reads the same perception vector and answers with three things: an orientation offset, an affinity, and a matter offset, one number per matter channel. Eight species, eight brains, eight answers.

— Combine. Now those answers have to become one cell.

The orientation offsets are averaged, each weighted by how much of that species is present, and the result turns the cell's direction. A cell that is mostly species three turns mostly the way species three wanted.

The affinities are not averaged. Each one belongs to its own species, and each species redistributes its own concentration according to its own affinity — its contents split among the nine neighbours in shares proportional to exp(beta·affinity) at each of them, scaled to sum to one. Eight species, eight independent transports, and not one of them creates or destroys anything. This is the reason they can stay distinct: a species is never carried by somebody else's decision.

The matter offsets are summed, weighted by concentration, and added to the ground. Then the matter is spread by diffuse and faded by decay.

That is the whole simulation.

What is deliberate

A species never senses another species. It senses the ground, and the ground is the only thing anyone writes to — the way an ant smells what has been left rather than the ants that left it. Every interaction in here is indirect, through matter, and a brain is free to lay down something that another brain runs from.

The direction is state. It is rotated by an offset and then carried along with the mass that moves, never read back out of where the mass happened to go.

The kernel is built from the angle, not rotated: its lobe centres are computed from this cell's own heading every step, so nothing is a stored stencil being turned and resampled. Each lobe is read as a blurred average over its own radius rather than as a scatter of point taps, which is what makes them smooth blobs instead of a handful of dots.
