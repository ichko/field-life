The state

Three things, and nothing else.

— Eight matter channels. Stuff lying on the ground. Deposited, spread and faded. Not conserved, and it must not be: fading is what makes a trail a trail.

— A direction per species. Two numbers a vector, nine vectors a cell — which way each species is facing here. Each one is turned only by its own brain and carried only by its own mass.

— Nine species channels. How much of each species is in this cell. Conserved, each one on its own.

The step

— Perceive. The kernel is turned to face the smelling species' own direction and convolved across all eight matter channels. Its lobes give a reading per channel, so what comes out is a perception vector. How many readings depends on the kernel setting: both eyes keeps each side separate for sixteen numbers, opposed eyes hands over only right minus left for eight, and eyes and centre adds the cell's own ground negated for twenty-four. Nine species facing nine ways means nine readings of the same ground.

— Think. Each species has a brain, and it is Fluoddity's: not a network with layers but a sum of ten random sinusoids. Each of the ten turns the whole perception into one phase and hands every output a different basis function of it — the fundamental and its first harmonic, in sine and cosine. Each brain reads its own perception vector and answers with three things: an orientation offset, an affinity, and a matter offset, one number per matter channel. Nine species, nine brains, nine answers.

Every centre draws its own frequency scale, biased low, so one brain comes out smooth and the next one jagged. That is the whole reason for the shape: a two-layer net with a fixed input scale gives every draw the same character however different its numbers are, which is exactly what resampling used to look like here. Gain dilates the phase — how many oscillations a perception travels through — and is the one knob that changes a run most.

— Combine. Now those answers have to become one cell.

The orientation offsets are not averaged. Each species carries its own heading, and its own offset turns only that heading — damped by how much of that species is standing here, so a heading is never spun about by a brain with nothing in the cell to do the smelling. Nine headings a cell, nine independent turns.

The affinities are not averaged. Each one belongs to its own species, and each species redistributes its own concentration according to its own affinity — its contents split among the nine neighbours in shares proportional to exp(beta·affinity) at each of them, scaled to sum to one. Nine species, nine independent transports, and not one of them creates or destroys anything. This is the reason they can stay distinct: a species is never carried by somebody else's decision.

The matter offsets are summed, weighted by concentration, and added to the ground. Then the matter is spread by diffuse and faded by decay.

That is the whole simulation.

What is deliberate

A species never senses another species. It senses the ground, and the ground is the only thing anyone writes to — the way an ant smells what has been left rather than the ants that left it. Every interaction in here is indirect, through matter, and a brain is free to lay down something that another brain runs from.

The direction is state, and there is one per species. It is rotated by that species' offset and carried along with that species' own mass — the weight a neighbour's heading arrives with is exactly how much of that species arrived with it. Never read back out of where the mass happened to go.

The kernel is built from the angle, not rotated: its lobe centres are computed from the smelling species' own heading every step, so nothing is a stored stencil being turned and resampled. Each lobe is read as a blurred average over its own radius rather than as a scatter of point taps, which is what makes them smooth blobs instead of a handful of dots.
